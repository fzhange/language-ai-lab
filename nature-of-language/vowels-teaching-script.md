# Teaching Script: Short and Long Vowel Sounds

## Opening Chant

**Teacher:**  
A, R, O, A, E, I, O, U, A, A, A, R, E, A.

**Teacher:**  
E, I, O, U, A, A, A, R, E, A.

**Teacher:**  
Oh! Now we know that vowels can have two sounds each—but they do not always sound the same.

## Short and Long E

**Teacher:**  
The short **e** says:

**Teacher and Students:**  
Eh, eh, eh, eh, eh, eh—**elephant**!

**Teacher:**  
The long **e** says:

**Teacher and Students:**  
Ee, ee, ee, ee, ee—**eagle**!

## Short and Long I

**Teacher:**  
The short **i** says:

**Teacher and Students:**  
Ih, ih, ih, ih, ih, ih—**insect**!

**Teacher:**  
The long **i** says:

**Teacher and Students:**  
Eye, eye, eye, eye, eye—**island**!

## Closing

**Teacher:**  
Great job learning the short and long vowel sounds!

**Teacher:**  
Let’s take a break.
