# The Old Man and the Lawn

Once upon a time, there was a lawn located in front of an old man's window. Children liked to play soccer on the lawn, so there was always noise outside the house.

But how could the old man fix this problem?

The old man told the children that he liked watching soccer and hoped they could come to play every day. In the meantime, he gave each child one buck as a reward. The children were happy, because they could do what they liked and earn money at the same time.

After a while, the old man told the children that he could only pay half a buck for each child. The children started to complain.

In the end, the old man told the children that he didn't have money to pay anymore, but he still hoped they could come to play soccer as usual. The children were unhappy, because they could not get any reward anymore, so they refused to continue playing soccer on the lawn.

This story actually does not want to tell you how to fix the noise issue. Soccer was a hobby for the children in the first place. Once the external reward was introduced, the children were no longer playing for their own joy — they were working for someone else. Once the reward system was introduced, the children's motivation disappeared. Extrinsic motivation killed intrinsic motivation.

- Back to real life, what's the purpose you earn money? 
- Freedom right? Finance freedom | Life freedom. 
- The reality is you give up the freedom for the money.
- The purpose of reading is you can have choice in the future. The real result is you gave up some choices for the reading.
- We always make a mistake between the purposes and the means. Finllay the means become the purpose. And the purpose becomes the means. In the first place, Money is the means, not the purpose. Freedom is the purpose, not the means. But in real life, money become the purpose, and freedom has totally disappeared.


- Summary

In this world, every one can deceive you excpet yourself. So at the very least, you should make sure not to deceive yourself.

## 语法修正说明

### 第一轮修正

| # | 原文 | 修正后 | 原因 |
|---|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Children **like** to play | Children **liked** to play | 全文是过去时态的故事，需保持时态一致 |
| 2 | So there **is** noise | so there **was** always noise | 同上，时态一致；同时用逗号连接前句更流畅 |
| 3 | **But how to fix** this problem **for** the old man? | But how **could** the old man fix this problem? | "how to fix" 缺少主语，改为完整疑问句 |
| 4 | he liked to **watch** soccer | he liked **watching** soccer | `like doing` 表示习惯性喜好，此处更地道 |
| 5 | hoped **them can come** | hoped **they could come** | `hope` 后接从句，主语用主格 **they**；过去时用 **could** |
| 6 | one buck as an **award** | one buck as a **reward** | **award** = 官方奖项；**reward** = 酬谢/报酬 |
| 7 | **cause** they could | **because** they could | `cause` 是口语缩写，书面写作用 **because** |
| 8 | **the things that they liked** | **what they liked** | 更简洁地道，避免冗余 |
| 9 | only pay **0.5 bucks** | only pay **half a buck** | 英语中说 "half a buck" 比 "0.5 bucks" 自然 |

### 第二轮修正

| # | 原文 | 修正后 | 原因 |
|---|------|--------|------|
| 10 | told ... that **i don't** have money | told ... that **he didn't** have money | 间接引语：主语变第三人称 he，时态退为过去时 didn't |
| 11 | **but i** still **hope** | but **he** still **hoped** | 同上，间接引语中人称和时态都要变 |
| 12 | **you guys** could come | **they** could come | 间接引语中 you → they |
| 13 | play soccer **continuously** | play soccer **as usual** | continuously = 24 小时不停；as usual = 像往常一样，语义准确 |
| 14 | **cannot** continue | **could not** continue | 过去时叙事，不能突然跳回现在时 cannot |
| 15 | could not get the reward **and** cannot continue | could not get any reward anymore, **so they refused to** continue | 拿不到钱是因，不来踢球是果，需要因果连接 |
| 16 | **The soccer** is **the** hobby | **Soccer** was **a** hobby | 运动名词前不加 the；was（过去时）；a hobby（不是唯一爱好） |
| 17 | motivation **was disappeared** | motivation **disappeared** | disappear 是不及物动词，不能用被动语态 |
| 18 | **the issues** | **the issue** | 前文只提了一个问题（噪音），用单数 |
