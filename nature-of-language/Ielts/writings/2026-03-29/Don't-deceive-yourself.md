# In one's lifetime, at the very least, one should make sure not to deceive oneself

> What's the meaning regarding our topic? During our lives, there are various deceptions. You literally cannot avoid them.

--- 

The first cheat is from your family. Your mother told you that you should study well.

- There is a gold house and a gorgeous one in books. If you read well, everything will definitely be perfect.

- After books, you should find a decent job. If you find one, everything will definitely be perfect.

- After the job, you should get a good wife. If you get one, everything will definitely be perfect. The foundation of a good marriage is that you have a house first. After everything is ready, your mother tells you that you should have a baby.

- Finally, you realize you are a willing ox. There are many shackles on your body. You feel tired. 

- Is your life better? 

- When you grow up, your leader tells you that you should not only consider the current situation. You will be rich in the future with the company's development. Each one in our company will be a millionaire or multimillionaire. You were convinced. After ten years, the company still has not developed well.

- You got tired of the current life, and you went abroad. You met a person like GuoWenGui who told you to buy XIBI. Okay, you followed the recommendations. You believed it would double two thousand times. Teacher Li told you to buy Li coin. The activist told you that you should do something radical to break the shackles. We should guard our freedom. You need to donate for freedom. We should see your efforts.

- Will you have a happy life? Everyone deceives you; at the very least, you should make sure not to deceive yourself.

--- 

There is a kind of promise that can never be fulfilled. But why do you never have any doubt? You believe your mother and everyone else except yourself. There are some reasons deep in your heart. It is actually a psychological problem.

- Once you start to doubt, every decision you made in the past seems wrong or not perfect. The things your mother told you, the efforts you made. Finally, you realize the things you did were not exactly right. They have gone forever — the time, the effort, and the youth. It's an incredibly complicated thing; if you don't believe in what you did, you are actually denying yourself. To keep believing your choices were right, you can only persist. Sometimes, you know they are cheating you, and there are lies. But you're afraid to face the truth. Your beliefs will collapse.

- The second reason is that the earlier people indeed got the benefits. Take our parents' generation as an example: they studied, read, and worked hard. They were assigned a house. Their salaries were raised. The price of the house indeed rose — maybe double, even triple. But there is a precondition: the return for the parents' generation is based on the contribution of the current generation. It uses the investment from latecomers to pay the benefits of the early birds. It's like a Ponzi scheme. So you need to tell a compelling story to attract new generations. Once the later generations break the rules, the game will be over.

--- 
Education and skills are experiencing the same thing now. The skills of an engineer could even last 15 years. Today, the half-life of skills in the same domain is 2.5 years. What does "half-life" mean? The value of what you learned is halved every 2.5 years. So you need to keep learning throughout your life. Do you need to go beyond others? The answer is no. The only thing you need to care about is not falling behind. Then you will ask: what is the meaning of life?

## 🔑 关键修改

### 1. 文化意象不能直译

| 原文 | 修改 | 原因 |
|------|------|------|
| a gold house and a gorgeous one | houses of gold and beauties of jade | "黄金屋""颜如玉"直译英文读者看不懂，加 "old Chinese saying" 给背景 |
| a willing ox | a beast of burden | "甘愿做牛"英文没这个意象，beast of burden = 负重的牲畜，是对应表达 |

### 2. 语法修正

| 原文 | 修改 | 规律 |
|------|------|------|
| there are various deceives | we encounter all kinds of deception | deceive 是动词，名词是 deception |
| You literally can not avoid it | You simply cannot avoid it | cannot 是一个词；literally 用太多会显得不地道 |
| the company still does not have good development | the company still shows no sign of success | 中式英语"有好的发展"→英文说 show signs of success |
| Will you have a happy life? | Will you ever find a happy life? | 加 ever 增强反问语气 |
| Let me have an example | Take... as an example | have an example 不是英文搭配，用 take...as an example |
| the half-life period | the half-life | 英文直接说 half-life，不需要加 period |

### 3. 连贯性提升

| 原文问题 | 修改方式 |
|----------|----------|
| 从"家庭欺骗"直接跳到"领导欺骗"，没有过渡 | 加 "Then comes the deception from your workplace" 过渡句 |
| 从"领导"跳到"出国"，断裂感强 | 加 "Feeling disillusioned, you go abroad" 用情感状态做桥梁 |
| 第二段开头突然讲心理原因，缺衔接 | 用反问 "But why do you never question it?" 自然引出分析 |

### 4. 词汇升级（6.5 水平适用）

| 原文 | 升级 | 值得记住 |
|------|------|----------|
| you tried the current life | feeling disillusioned | disillusioned = 幻灭的，非常精准描述"对现状失望" |
| the things you did are not right exactly | what you did was not entirely right | entirely = 完全地，比 exactly 更准确 |
| your faiths will collapse | your belief system would collapse | belief system 是固定搭配，比 faiths 更准确 |
| the front people | the early participants / early birds | "前面的人"英文说 early participants 或 early birds |
| a later person | latecomers | latecomer = 后来者，一个词搞定 |
| fake an excellent story | a compelling story | compelling = 有说服力的，fake a story 搭配不当 |

## 💡 三个核心提升方向

1. **动词名词要分清** — deceive(v.) vs deception(n.)，这类错误在雅思中扣分很重
2. **告别中式英语** — "have good development" → "show signs of success"，多积累英文的固定搭配
3. **加过渡句** — 段落之间加 then / feeling... / but why... 这类衔接，CC 分数会明显提升
