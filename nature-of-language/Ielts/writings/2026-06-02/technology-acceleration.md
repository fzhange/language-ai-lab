# Pushed by Technology

> 主题：科技迭代的加速正在剥夺普通开发者"慢下来"的权利。

---

## 📝 原文

From technology to philosophy, the iteration of technology is faster and faster. We are facing a technological revolution.

Technological accumunlation is not exist for most normal developers. At first, people should be slow to enjoy the convenience of technology. However, we are being pushed constantly by technology to pursue acceleration.

Unwilling to slow down a little bit or have more thoughts and ideas collide.

---

## ✏️ 修改后版本

From technology to philosophy, every field is iterating ever faster. We are in the middle of a technological revolution.

Yet for most ordinary developers, real technical accumulation no longer exists. Ideally, people ought to savor the convenience technology brings, at their own pace. Instead, we are constantly pushed by technology itself to keep accelerating —— unwilling to slow down even a little, unwilling to let new ideas have the time to collide.

## 🔬 本质性问题精讲

### 问题 1：`is not exist` —— "是 + 动词原形"是中式英语第一陷阱

**根源**：中文"**是不存在的**"被逐字翻译。

```
中文： 积累        是      不存在的
        ↓          ↓        ↓
英文： accumulation  is    not exist   ← 致命错误
```

**揭示本质：** `be` 是**状态系动词**，后面只能接：

- 形容词：`is happy`
- 名词：`is a problem`
- 介词短语：`is in trouble`
- 分词：`is going` / `is broken`

**它绝对不能直接接动词原形** (`exist` / `know` / `agree` / `disappear`)。

要表达"不存在"，三条路径对应三种语义重心：

| 你想强调 | 句式 | 例 |
|---------|------|----|
| **动作不发生**（中性） | `does not exist` | `Such accumulation does not exist for...` |
| **从无到有的缺失** | `there is no + 名词` | `There is no such accumulation...` |
| **主语本身被否定** | `no + 名词 + 谓语` | `No such accumulation exists...` |

> 💡 **判别规律**：看到"是 + 动词"的中文句子，先停 1 秒，问自己——这个动词需要主语自己来"做"吗？需要 → 用 `do/does + V`；不需要 → 转成名词或形容词。

### 问题 2：`be slow to do` —— 过程轴定位错误

**原句：** `people should be slow to enjoy the convenience of technology.`

你想说"慢慢享受"，但 `be slow to do` 锁定的位置和你想的**完全相反**。

#### 画出过程轴：

```
   迟迟不做 ─────► 终于开始做 ─────► 做的过程中 ─────► 快速做完 ─────► 一晃而过
   ★(slow to do)                    (do slowly)        (do quickly)     (rush through)
   英文锁定点                        中文"慢慢做"锁定点
```

| 表达 | 锁定段位 | 含义 |
|------|---------|------|
| `be slow to do` | ★ 动作开始**之前**的延迟段 | **不情愿、拖延**才做（贬义） |
| `do sth slowly` | 动作进行中的速度 | 慢悠悠地做 |
| `savor / take one's time` | 动作进行中的**主观沉浸** | 慢慢品味（褒义） |

**对照证据：**

- `He was slow to apologize.` = 他迟迟不肯道歉。（**不是**"他慢慢道歉"）
- `She was slow to trust strangers.` = 她不轻易信任陌生人。

🔑 所以原句 `people should be slow to enjoy` 在母语者读来是：
> "人们应该**不情愿享受**科技的便利。" ← 与本意完全相反！

**正确锁定 ★ 享受过程：**

```
✅ people ought to savor the convenience technology brings.
✅ people deserve the time to enjoy technology at their own pace.
✅ people should be allowed to take their time enjoying...
```

### 问题 3：`At first` ≠ "按理说"

`at first` 锁定的是**时间轴上的第一段**：

```
   At first ───► then ───► later ───► finally
   起初              然后        后来           最后
```

`at first` = "起初是 A，后来变成 B" 的**时间对比起点**。例：
*At first I hated it, but later I came to love it.*

而"按理说"是**逻辑/期望**的范畴，不是时间：

| 中文 | 英文 | 锁定的语义维度 |
|------|------|---------------|
| 起初 | At first | 时间起点 |
| 按理说 / 本应 | **Ideally** / **In theory** / **By rights** | 期望 vs 现实的对比 |
| 一开始我以为 | Initially I thought | 认知起点 |

### 问题 4：`normal` vs `ordinary` —— 语义对立面不同

```
   abnormal ──── normal ──── ordinary ──── average ──── exceptional
   不正常         正常的       普通的         一般的         卓越的
                 (vs 异常)    (vs 杰出)      (vs 突出)
```

`normal` 的对立面是 **abnormal**（异常、不正常），带医学/心理学色彩。说 `normal developers` 暗示"还有不正常的开发者"。

要表达"绝大多数**普通**开发者"，对立面是"杰出/明星开发者"——

- ✅ `most ordinary developers`
- ✅ `the average developer`（单数泛指）

---

### 问题 5：`pursue acceleration` —— 抽象名词中式化

`acceleration` 是物理学术语（加速度）。把它当 `pursue` 的宾语，读起来像论文摘要。

**揭示本质：** 英语偏爱**动词驱动**，中文偏爱**名词堆砌**。

| 中式 | 英式 |
|------|------|
| pursue acceleration | keep accelerating |
| achieve the improvement of efficiency | improve efficiency |
| make a contribution to the development | contribute to development |

> 💡 **写作时自查**：如果你写出 `do / make / pursue / achieve + 抽象名词`，先问能不能直接把名词改回动词。

---

### 问题 6：最后一句是**句子片段** (sentence fragment)

**原句：** `Unwilling to slow down a little bit or have more thoughts and ideas collide.`

- 没有主语和谓语动词，只是形容词短语挂在那儿
- `thoughts and ideas` **语义重复**（思想 ≈ 想法）
- `have ideas collide` 偏使役（强迫让其发生），思想碰撞应该是 `let ideas collide`（任由其发生）

**两条修复路径：**

```
路径 A：补全主谓
✅ We are unwilling to slow down even a little, or to let ideas collide.

路径 B：连到上一句作伴随状语
✅ ...pushed to keep accelerating, unwilling to slow down even a little
   or to let new ideas have the time to collide.
```

## 💡 三条带走的规律

1. **看到"是 + 动词"的中文，立刻警觉** —— 90% 要转成 `do/does + V` 或 `there is no + N`
2. **形容词 + to do 时，先查它锁定动作的哪一段** —— `slow to do` 是延迟段，不是过程段
3. **抽象名词当宾语前，问能不能直接用动词** —— `pursue acceleration` → `keep accelerating`
