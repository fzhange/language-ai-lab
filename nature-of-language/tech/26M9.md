## offload 
> v **卸载、外移、转交**
> - **Retrieve, offload, and delegate**: Offload retrieved document chunks onto the agent's filesystem backend rather than cluttering the orchestrator context, allowing specialized subagents to analyze them in parallel.
>
> - cluttering the orchestrator context  **把编排器上下文弄得杂乱臃肿** 
> - “cluttering” 最常见的意思是使杂乱、堆满，是动词 clutter 的现在分词 / 动名词形式，常表示东西过多挤占空间，造成混乱。
