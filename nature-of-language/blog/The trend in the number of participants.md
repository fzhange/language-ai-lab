The line chart shows how the trend in the number of participants changes for five diverse activities (Film club, Martial arts, Amateur dramatics, Table tennis, and Musical performances) at one social centre in Melbourne from 2000 to 2020.

In 2000, the number of participants in the Film club was the highest among the five activities, nearly 65 people. The next was Martial arts, Amateur dramatics, Table tennis, and Musical performances, sorted by the number of participants; The numbers were 35, 27, 18, and 0, one by one. 

It was worth noticing — there was no one to choose from the musical performances activity between 2000 and 2005, but it changed after 2005. The number started to climb, then reached the peak in 2020 (around 19 people).

By contrast, the number of people in Amateur dramatics fell after 2005; The number of peak was in 2005, which was around 19 people. The number of participants in Table tennis increased rapidly from 2010 to 2020, from 20 to around 53. The line of Martial arts remained stable during 20 years from 2000 to 2020 — 31 (minimum number) - 38 (maximum number). In the meantime, no activity was higher than the Film club from 2000 to 2020; it was always the favorite activity loved by people.
